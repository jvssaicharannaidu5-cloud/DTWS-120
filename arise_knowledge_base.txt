## 14. COMMON QUESTIONS
What is the Digital Twin?
It is a virtual representation of the well and pumping system used to visualize simulated telemetry, analyze performance, detect anomalies, and test optimization scenarios.

What is BGW-04?
BGW-04 is the default demonstration well in the fictional field dataset.

What is SRP?
SRP stands for Sucker Rod Pump, represented as a pumping-system model in the Digital Twin.

What is CSS?
CSS stands for Cyclic Steam Stimulation. The platform models steam injection, soaking, production, and monitoring.

Is the telemetry real?
The platform uses simulated telemetry unless a verified live data connection is explicitly shown.

Can the AI control the well?
No. The prototype provides simulation and decision support. It does not directly control industrial equipment.

What can AI detect?
Configured simulated anomalies such as pump-load deviations, efficiency degradation, abnormal steam parameters, production deviations, and telemetry issues.

What can AI optimize?
It can simulate changes to stroke length, SPM, pump speed, steam settings, and CSS timing.
